Type: prototype
Status: open
Role: World
Slot: T+0:25 to 1:15
Blocked by: 01

## Question

What does the arcade look like, and how does moving between Room mode and Play mode feel? Build: palette (Roboto-flavoured, 5 to 7 flat colours), lighting, floor/walls/signage, the three cabinet silhouettes plus the Store counter footprint, camera fly-to on click with Escape to return, and the HUD shell (station name, "press to play" prompt, Ticket balance). World owns the camera; Machines never touch it.

Feedback from the other two roles at checkpoint 1. Answer records: the locked palette, camera dock positions per station, the interaction rules. Merged to `main` as the base everyone integrates into.
